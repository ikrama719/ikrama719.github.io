    Experience

    During my two months of work experience with NHS field engineers, I gained valuable hands-on exposure to the day-to-day responsibilities of supporting and maintaining IT systems within a healthcare environment. I observed and assisted with the installation, configuration, and maintenance of a wide range of hardware and equipment used across NHS sites, helping me understand the importance of reliable technology in delivering effective healthcare services.

    Throughout the placement, I supported engineers with troubleshooting technical issues, diagnosing faults, and ensuring equipment was functioning correctly. This gave me practical experience in identifying problems, following logical processes to find solutions, and working under pressure to minimise disruption for staff. I also gained experience with desktop rollouts, hardware replacements, device setups, and configuring BIOS settings on computers. In addition, I developed a basic understanding of networking concepts and how devices connect and communicate within a large organisational infrastructure. Working alongside experienced field engineers allowed me to see how technical knowledge, attention to detail, and effective planning are used to complete tasks efficiently and safely. The experience significantly improved my troubleshooting, communication, and teamwork skills. I regularly interacted with IT staff and observed how engineers communicated technical information clearly and professionally to users with varying levels of technical knowledge. Overall, the placement strengthened my confidence in an IT environment, enhanced my technical skills, and confirmed my interest in pursuing a career related to field engineering and technology.
